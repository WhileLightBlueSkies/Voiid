## 1. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

- **Rules notified:** November 2025. Most duties, including children's data, apply from **13 May 2027**.
- **Child:** anyone under 18.
- **Parent consent:** Voiid needs verifiable consent from a parent before processing a child's data (Rule 10).
- **Checking the parent (Rule 10):** Voiid must confirm the parent is an identifiable adult (18+), using either:
  - reliable identity and age details Voiid already holds. The rule's examples cover a parent who is already a registered user; or
  - details or a virtual token from a government-authorised source, such as a DigiLocker service provider.
- **Limits on processing children's data:**
  - no processing likely to harm a child's well-being
  - no tracking or behavioural monitoring
  - no targeted advertising aimed at children
- **Exceptions:** essential services like healthcare, education and real-time safety. Voiid doesn't fall under these.
- **Penalties:** up to ₹200 crore for breaking children's-data obligations.
- **Other duties:** clear notice, deletion rights, a contact person for data questions, breach notification.

### What this means for Voiid

- Voiid currently holds only a phone number, name, username and optional email. That isn't reliable age information, so parents need a one-time check, e.g. a DigiLocker age token.
- Every Indian user under 18 needs a parent-linked account by 13 May 2027.
- For under-18s: no ads, no profiling, non-personalized feed.

## 2. IT Rules 2021 (updated 10 Feb 2026)

### 2a. As a platform where users post

- **Grievance Officer:** name, contact details and how to complain, shown prominently. "Prominently" means on the home screen, or on a screen reached directly from it.
- **Timelines:**

| Complaint | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Any complaint | Acknowledge within 24 hours; resolve within 7 days |
| Certain removal requests | 36 hours |
| Content showing a person's private areas, nudity or sexual acts | 2 hours |

- **Bigger platforms** (significant social media intermediaries; the government-notified threshold is 50 lakh registered users in India) have extra duties:
  - a Chief Compliance Officer, Nodal Contact Person and Resident Grievance Officer
  - asking users whether their content is AI-made, verifying that, and labelling it

### 2b. As publisher of Voiid's own series and clips

- **Online curated content:** a curated catalogue of audio-visual content, other than news, that is owned by, licensed to or contracted to the publisher and offered on demand. Voiid's official series and clips fit this.
- **Self-rating:** the publisher classifies its own content. No CBFC certificate is needed.
- **Categories:**

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| U | Suitable for all ages |
| U/A 7+ | 7 and above; under 7 with parental guidance |
| U/A 13+ | 13 and above; under 13 with parental guidance |
| U/A 16+ | 16 and above; under 16 with parental guidance |
| A | Adults only |

- **Content descriptors:** themes and messages, violence, nudity, sex, language, drugs and substance abuse, horror.
- **Display:** show the rating and descriptors at the start of every programme, before viewing. Also include them in promos.
- **Parental locks:** U/A 13+ and above must have access controls, including parental locks.
- **A-rated content:** needs a reliable age verification mechanism.
- **Age filters count:** the rules define an "access control mechanism" as any measure restricting access based on verified identity or age, so filtering by age band counts.
- **Grievance Officer:** publishers need one based in India, with contact details displayed.
- **Code of Ethics:** publishers should take care with content about any racial or religious group, given India's multi-religious context. This matters for Akbar–Birbal, Jataka Tales and similar series.

### 2c. AI-generated content

- **What counts:** "synthetically generated information" is AI-made or AI-altered content that looks real and shows people or events in a way likely to seem real.
- **What doesn't:** routine editing, colour correction, translation, accessibility work, and illustrative educational material.
- **If Voiid offers AI creation tools:** content made with them must carry a visible label, plus permanent metadata where technically possible.
- **Bigger platforms:** must ask users to declare AI content, verify it, and label it.
- **Voiid's plan:** a "Made with AI" tag on all AI-made content.

## 3. India checklist

- [ ] Grievance Officer named; name and contact on the home screen or one screen away
- [ ] Complaint queue with 24h / 36h / 2h / 7-day timers
- [ ] Rating plus descriptors before each official episode; all official content at U or U/A 7+
- [ ] Birth date screen at signup
- [ ] Parent-linked accounts for all Indian under-18s before 13 May 2027
- [ ] DigiLocker (or equivalent) age check for parents
- [ ] No ads, profiling or personalized feed for under-18s
- [ ] "Made with AI" tag
- [ ] Ask a lawyer what else a publisher of curated content must do
