## Part 1: European Union

### Summary

| Law | What it requires | In force |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR Article 8 | Parent consent below the country's digital consent age (13–16); reasonable efforts to verify it | Now |
| Digital Services Act, Article 28 | Platforms used by minors must ensure a high level of privacy, safety and security; no ads based on profiling minors | Now; Commission guidelines 14 Jul 2025 |
| AI Act, Article 50 | Disclose AI-generated or AI-altered content | 2 Aug 2026 |

### 1. GDPR: digital age of consent by country

Below this age, a parent must consent when an online service relies on consent to process a child's data.

| Country | Age | Country | Age |
|---|---|---|---|
| Austria | 14 | Italy | 14 |
| Belgium | 13 | Latvia | 13 |
| Bulgaria | 14 | Lithuania | 14 |
| Croatia | 16 | Luxembourg | 16 |
| Cyprus | 14 | Malta | 13 |
| Czech Republic | 15 | Netherlands | 16 |
| Denmark | 13 | Poland | 16 |
| Estonia | 13 | Portugal | 13 |
| Finland | 13 | Romania | 16 |
| France | 15 | Slovakia | 16 |
| Germany | 16 | Slovenia | 16 (15 proposed) |
| Greece | 15 | Spain | 14 |
| Hungary | 16 | Sweden | 13 |
| Ireland | 16 | | |

Sources disagree for a few countries (e.g. France, Ireland, Spain). Confirm with a lawyer.

- **Verification:** the service must make "reasonable efforts" to verify parent consent, "taking into consideration available technology".
- **For Voiid:** the global rule (parent link for all under-18s) covers every country age. Messaging with minors is higher risk, so use a real parent check, not just codes.

### 2. Digital Services Act (DSA)

- **Applies to Voiid:** Clips lets users post publicly, which likely makes Voiid an "online platform". Ask the lawyer to confirm.
- **Article 28:** platforms accessible to minors must ensure a high level of privacy, safety and security, and must not show ads based on profiling to users they know are minors.
- **Commission guidelines (14 Jul 2025):** not binding, but used to judge compliance. They cover:
  - effective age assurance, with the EU's own age verification app as the benchmark
  - safe registration and default settings
  - design that doesn't push compulsive use
  - recommender systems
  - commercial practices
  - reporting and moderation
- **Other DSA duties:** notice-and-action reporting, telling users why content was removed, and a legal representative in the EU if Voiid has no EU office.

### 3. AI Act, Article 50 (since 2 Aug 2026)

- Content that is AI-generated or AI-altered to look real must be disclosed.
- For clearly artistic, creative or fictional work, the disclosure can be light and must not spoil viewing.
- Voiid's "Made with AI" tag covers this.

### 4. EU checklist

- [ ] Parent-linked accounts for under-18s (covers every country's age)
- [ ] Parent check stronger than codes (age estimation or ID)
- [ ] No profiling-based ads for minors
- [ ] Private, safe defaults for minors (see [Global – Safest Baseline](Global%20-%20Safest%20Baseline.md))
- [ ] Reporting flow with reasons given for removals
- [ ] "Made with AI" tag
- [ ] Data protection impact assessment for children's features
- [ ] EU GDPR representative and DSA legal representative, if Voiid has no EU office

## Part 2: United Kingdom (separate from the EU)

### Summary

| Law | What it requires | In force |
|---|---|---|
| UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018 | Digital consent age 13 | Now |
| Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code) | Services likely used by under-18s must follow its standards | Now |
| Online Safety Act 2023 | Child safety duties for services where users interact or post | Protection of Children Codes since 25 Jul 2025 |

### 1. Children's Code

Applies to online services likely to be used by under-18s. Key standards for Voiid:
- Act in the child's best interests
- Do a data protection impact assessment
- High-privacy settings by default
- Collect the minimum data
- Location off by default
- Profiling off by default
- Tell children when parents can see their activity
- No nudges toward weaker privacy settings

### 2. Online Safety Act

- **Applies to Voiid:** messaging, communities and Clips are services where users interact or post, so they're covered.
- **Duties:**
  - assess the risk of illegal content and the risk to children
  - protect children from harmful content
  - provide reporting and complaints
- **Age checks:** "highly effective age assurance" is required to keep children away from the most harmful content (e.g. pornography, suicide, self-harm, eating disorder content).
- **Penalties:** up to £18 million or 10% of global revenue. Ofcom enforces.

### 3. UK checklist

- [ ] Birth date screen; age 13 minimum; parent-linked teens (global rule)
- [ ] Children's Code defaults: private, location off, profiling off
- [ ] Illegal content and children's risk assessments
- [ ] Reporting and complaints flow
- [ ] UK GDPR representative, if Voiid has no UK office
